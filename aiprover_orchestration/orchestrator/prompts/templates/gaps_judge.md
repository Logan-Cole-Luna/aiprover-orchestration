Lemmas named `gap_...` are steps the draft names as open. Do not reject the sketch because a gap is unproved. A gap is a blocking issue only if it is equivalent to or stronger than the theorem, or is a known open problem that the theorem does not reduce to; a gap you believe false is a suspect. A gap that could be split into a routine part and a smaller open part, or a step with a known argument stated as a gap, is a note: gaps are attempted after the routine lemmas and split when provers get stuck. Judge every other lemma as usual.
