
Open steps. If the informal proof has a step that no known argument proves, state that step as its own lemma and name it `gap_...`. A gap lemma must be strictly weaker than the theorem: the theorem with an extra hypothesis, one case of a case split, or a claim about a smaller object, never the theorem restated. Keep each gap as narrow as the proof allows, and keep every step that has an argument out of the gap lemmas, as ordinary lemmas. The routine lemmas are proved first and the gaps afterwards. A gap is never assumed: it remains a proof target, split and revised until it is proved.
